Inka Guba08 — Research & discovery

The redesign started with listening, not a moodboard.

innaguba.com was relaunching after a long pause, with a low conversion rate. Before touching a single pixel, the real question was where the problem actually lived — unclear navigation, not enough emotion coming through, or friction somewhere inside the path from first visit to booked session.

Three questions shaped the whole research phase: what actually triggers trust and desire in a parent looking at this work, where do people drop off, and do they understand — and value — the difference between a photo and a video story enough to pay for it.

Method
  • 3 in-depth interviews
    existing & prospective clients
  • Affinity diagram
    pain points, delights, suggested solutions
  • 1 working persona
    Tereza — grounds every decision in one real person
  • Prototype & usability testing
    think-aloud, 2 participants
Problem statement

„Create a clear site and an intuitive user flow that naturally carries a parent from first impression to booking — and remove whatever is still standing in the way.“

Inka Guba10 — Persona
The caregiver · "one of us"

Tereza

35 · works part-time · husband & 2 kids (2 & 6) · village near Brno

A conscious mother who rejects the "perfect Instagram mom" image even as she scrolls it for inspiration. A minimalist with good taste who values fairness, warmth and punctuality. Her days are full of love and the ordinary chaos of small kids — and a real urgency to hold on to this time before they're grown.

Needs

Keep the memories without the stress of posing. Someone who can capture her kids exactly as they are.

Values

An artistic eye over heavy commercial retouching — photos that still feel like her family, not a catalogue.

Frustrations

Worries the shoot will feel forced, the kids won't cooperate, and the result won't match what she pictured.

What she needs from this site

Proof this won't be another thing to perform for — before she ever has to ask.

Inka Guba11 — Prototype & testing

Tested a full booking flow — shipped something simpler

A 4-step calendar booking prototype was built and usability-tested first, with two participants thinking aloud through it on mobile. The interaction patterns that held up — clear CTA labels, price shown before commitment, no dead ends — carried into the final site's single enquiry form.

What held up
  • Smooth flow end to end — the mobile form never stalled either participant.
  • Preselecting the chosen package carried it into step 1, no repeat input.
  • Large, separate tap targets for time slots read as fast and obvious.
What got fixed
  • "Pokračovat" on the final step submitted instantly — one participant was caught off guard. Renamed to make the final action unmistakable.
  • No way to go back a step without restarting — added back navigation.
  • Location preference (studio / outdoors) had to be typed into a free-text note — flagged for a dedicated field.
Inka Guba12 — Overview

A website that feels like a family album

Inka Guba photographs and films families and children in Brno. After twelve years and 900+ families, her work had outgrown a template site that looked like everyone else's.

The goal was a site that feels the way her photographs do — warm, honest and unhurried — while quietly guiding parents from a first feeling to a booked session. No stock phrases, no noise. Just real moments, room to breathe and one clear next step.

Scope
  • Creative direction
  • UX & site structure
  • Copywriting
  • UI & visual system
  • Responsive front-end
  • Photo & video curation
  • SEO & performance
Inka Guba13 — Homepage

One story, top to bottom

The homepage reads like a conversation with a parent: an emotional promise, proof, the worry everyone has (“what if my kids won't cooperate?”), what you get, what it costs, how it works — and only then the ask. Dark and light sections alternate to set the rhythm.

Inka Guba15 — Mobile first

Designed for the phone in one hand

Most parents find the site on mobile — often with a child in the other arm. Everything sits on one calm centre axis.
