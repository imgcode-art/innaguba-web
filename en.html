Prices and questions

1. Do you speak English?+

Yes, fluently. I photograph in English, Czech, Ukrainian and Russian, so the whole session – from the first message to the final gallery – can be in English. Many of my clients are international and expat families living in Brno.

2. What kind of photography do you offer?+

Family photography, maternity (pregnancy) sessions, newborn photography, child portraits and short family films. Every session can include photos, video, or both.

3. How much does a family photo session in Brno cost?+

Small session: 1 hour, 20 edited photos, 5,900 CZK. Large session: 2 hours, 60 edited photos, 7,500 CZK. Photos and video: 2 hours, 60 photos plus a 1–1.5 minute film, 12,400 CZK. Family video on its own from 6,900 CZK. Child portrait: 45 minutes, 20 edited photos, 3,900 CZK. Maternity and newborn sessions follow the family session prices.

4. Where do the sessions take place?+

Outdoors in Brno and the South Moravian region – a meadow, a park, by the water or your favourite place – in my studio at Pellicova 19 in the centre of Brno, or at your home.

5. What is your style?+

Natural and unposed. Children do not have to sit still or smile on command and you do not need perfectly matching outfits. I use a silent shutter, take breaks whenever the kids need one and simply capture what happens between you.

6. When will we get our photos?+

Within 3–4 weeks of the session, in a private online gallery, in both colour and black and white.

7. How far in advance should we book?+

Ideally about 4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends. Write to me even at short notice – I always try to find a way. I reply within 24 hours.
